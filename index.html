Filmmaking FAQs

Yes. I am available for directing short films, feature film scripts in development, music videos, and commercial narrative projects.

Yes! Most of my short films (*7 Minutes*, *In Between*, *Hope*) are original screenplays I wrote and developed. I also collaborate with writers to direct existing scripts.

Development and pre-production usually take 3 to 6 weeks, production runs over several shooting days, and post-production (editing, color grading, sound design) takes 4 to 8 weeks.
